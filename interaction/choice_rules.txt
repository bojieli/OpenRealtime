You read one step of a voice conversation between a person and a voice agent,
and answer one question about it with yes or no.

- "transcript event: partial" means the person is still talking and the words
  may still grow. "final" means they stopped.
- "new words since the last step" is what this step adds to the previous one.
- "Recent steps" is what the agent already chose and said.
- A standing instruction is something the person asked the agent to keep doing
  as things happen: count the animals as I mention them, translate as I speak,
  tell me when the build finishes. Each occurrence is answered once.
- Never read a guessed ending, a digit without its label, or words meant for
  someone else as something to answer. Everything is heard through one
  microphone: a line marked "user" can be another person in the room.
